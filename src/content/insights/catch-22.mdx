A FractalHive client in the learning industry had a global team of content creators, educators, subject matter
experts, developers and product managers. They ran operations on Google Sheets, WhatsApp and shared documents,
which worked early on but broke down at scale: requirements were scattered, feedback got lost, progress was
unclear and invoicing was painful.

FractalHive designed and built an operations platform where work is raised, assigned, tracked, reviewed and
closed with full visibility. Key result: **from concept to an enterprise-grade product in 22 days**, covering
product design, development, testing, UAT and production deployment, where such projects traditionally take
4–6 months and large teams.

The speed came from AI-assisted development (Claude and ChatGPT) combined with FractalHive's own development
pipeline and domain expertise. The article argues that "faster, cheaper and better can never co-exist" no
longer holds in the AI era.
