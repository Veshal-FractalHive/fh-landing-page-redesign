We recently came across an Economic Times article stating that "India's organised gold loan market could exceed ₹30 lakh crore by FY28, growing at nearly 28% CAGR." While the growth opportunity is significant, it raises an equally important question.

As more banks and NBFCs expand into Gold Loans, is the industry equally prepared from a collateral governance perspective?

Growth is only one part of the story. The real challenge is managing collateral risk at scale.

As portfolios grow, institutions need answers to a few critical questions:

- Are LTV positions monitored continuously or only periodically?
- Can emerging collateral shortfalls be identified before they become portfolio risks?
- Is collateral visibility available across borrowers with multiple facilities?
- Can Credit and Risk teams respond quickly when market conditions change?

There is another challenge that often goes unnoticed. What if a bank or NBFC decides to launch a Gold Loan business today?

While the business opportunity may be immediate, building the supporting technology—collateral governance, LTV monitoring, valuation workflows, audit controls, and portfolio visibility—can take several months.

In a competitive market, speed of execution matters as much as product innovation.

Instead of waiting for lengthy core system enhancements, institutions can accelerate operational readiness by deploying a collateral intelligence layer that integrates with existing lending systems. This enables policy-driven collateral governance, continuous LTV monitoring, portfolio-wide visibility, and audit readiness within weeks rather than months.

The next competitive advantage in secured lending will not simply be originating more loans. It will be the ability to scale lending with stronger governance, continuous collateral intelligence, and real-time portfolio visibility.
