Effective Date: August 7, 2026

FractalHive Solutions Private Limited ("FractalHive," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our customers, partners, website visitors, and users of our products and services.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, store, process, and safeguard information when you visit our website or use our software products and services, including but not limited to V-Track, NotifAI, ElevateRM, and any related applications, websites, APIs, and support services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with our Services, we may collect the following categories of information:

### Contact Information

When you contact us, request a demonstration, register for our services, or communicate with us, we may collect information such as:

- Name
- Business email address
- Company name
- Job title
- Telephone number
- Business address

### Account Information

Where applicable, we may collect information required to create and manage user accounts, including authentication credentials, user roles, and account preferences.

### Technical Information

When you access our website or applications, we may automatically collect technical information including:

- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Device information
- Log files
- Usage analytics
- Session information
- Diagnostic and performance data

### Customer Data

Our enterprise applications may process business information provided by our customers in connection with their use of our Services. This information is processed solely to deliver the contracted services and remains under the ownership and control of the respective customer.

### Support Information

We may collect information provided when you contact our support team, submit inquiries, report issues, or request technical assistance.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Deliver, operate, and maintain our products and services.
- Improve the functionality, reliability, and performance of our platforms.
- Authenticate users and manage secure access.
- Provide customer support and technical assistance.
- Communicate product updates, service notifications, and important operational information.
- Monitor platform usage, security, and system health.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent unauthorized access, fraud, and security incidents.
- Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

## 3. Customer Data

FractalHive acts as a technology service provider for customer data processed within our enterprise applications.

Our customers retain ownership of all customer data processed through our platforms. FractalHive processes such data solely in accordance with applicable contractual agreements, documented customer instructions, and applicable law. We do not use customer business data for purposes unrelated to providing our contracted services.

## 4. Data Security

Protecting customer information is a fundamental priority for FractalHive.

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction. These safeguards include, where applicable:

- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Secure authentication mechanisms
- Role-based access controls
- Security logging and monitoring
- Network and infrastructure protection
- Regular security assessments and reviews
- Secure software development practices

While we strive to protect all information entrusted to us, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Data Sharing

FractalHive Solutions does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We may disclose information only where necessary to:

- Trusted service providers supporting the delivery and operation of our Services.
- Cloud infrastructure providers hosting our applications.
- Regulatory authorities, law enforcement agencies, or government bodies where disclosure is required by applicable law.
- Professional advisors, auditors, or legal counsel where necessary for legitimate business purposes.

All third parties receiving information are expected to maintain appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

## 6. International Data Transfers

Depending on the services provided and customer deployment preferences, information may be processed in data centers located in multiple jurisdictions.

Where international data transfers occur, FractalHive Solutions implements appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable data protection and privacy laws.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to:

- Deliver our contracted services.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
- Resolve disputes.
- Enforce contractual rights and obligations.
- Maintain appropriate business records.

When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted, anonymized, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable policies and legal requirements.

## 8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Improve user experience.
- Maintain website functionality.
- Analyze website usage and performance.
- Enhance security.
- Remember user preferences.

Users may control or disable cookies through their browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.
